PokeUDS now comes with firmware **esp32-uds-bridge 2.1-uds**, the 3DS side on its own: the board no longer restarts
when PokeUDS connects, so its USB port stays put, which also fixes PokeUDS losing the board on a Mac. Update from 0.1.7
with the green **Update** entry at the foot of the left rail; if it fails, download `PokeUDS-windows.zip` and unzip it
over your `PokeUDS` folder (your `Bank`, `Data` and `received` folders are not in the zip and stay as they are). Then
install the new firmware from the **Board** page.

## New

- **Firmware 2.1-uds is included** for the ESP32-S3, C3, C6 and (alpha) the classic ESP32. VC-Adapter's firmware 2.1
  comes in three lines:
  - **uds** (the one PokeUDS installs): the 3DS side alone, for PokeUDS and Azahar. It boots in 3DS mode, so connecting
    never restarts the board.
  - **ldn**: what 2.0 called uds, with GB-Link Switch LDN's Switch side too, for mGBA-LDN. It still works with
    PokeUDS: the board restarts into its 3DS mode when PokeUDS connects, as before. The Board page offers 2.1-uds as
    an **optional update**; keep ldn if mGBA-LDN uses the board's Switch side.
  - **aio**: VC-Adapter's standalone board, as before.

  The Board page and `pokeuds board check` name the line (a 2.0-uds board shows as "uds line, 3DS and Switch sides
  (2.1 calls it ldn)").
- **A Mac no longer loses the board when it restarts.** A board with the Switch side drops its USB port when it
  switches to its 3DS mode, and macOS could bring it back under another `/dev/cu.usbmodem...` name, so PokeUDS kept
  waiting on the old one. PokeUDS now finds the board again by its USB serial number (the chip's MAC), even when you
  chose the port by hand. It never moves to a different board this way.
- **X / Y:** when PokeUDS hosts and the 3DS leaves the trade network before the connection is up, the run now ends
  ("the 3DS left the trade network") instead of waiting until stopped by hand. Sun / Moon inherits it. PokeUDS's
  trainer also shows a different avatar on the 3DS.
- **The left rail** reads Trade, Gifts, GTS, Bank, and Settings sits at the very bottom of the window.

## Not tested yet

- Firmware 2.1-uds on a board: 2.1-ldn, built from the same source with the Switch side added, has traded with
  PokeUDS on an ESP32-S3.
- PokeUDS on a Mac with a board on the ldn line (the case the USB serial number fix is for).
- Still from 0.1.7: Gen 5 gifts and the builder's Gen 4 Pokemon and Gen 5 cards sent by PokeUDS itself; PokeUDS
  hosting the Time Capsule; Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire and Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon on a console; the classic ESP32;
  online trades and Touch Trade between two real 3DSs.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- After an X / Y trade, choosing to register PokeUDS's trainer as a friend fails on the 3DS: PokeUDS has no friend
  data to give it.
- Flashing any firmware erases the keys a board stores. PokeUDS doesn't use them, but Azahar and uds-esp32-setup users
  have to store theirs again.
- A custom card's Pokemon matches no real event, so PKHeX flags it ("Unable to match to a Mystery Gift").
- The Windows app is unsigned: Windows may warn about an unknown publisher on first start (More info, Run anyway).
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
