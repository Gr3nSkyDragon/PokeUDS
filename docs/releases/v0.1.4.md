PokeUDS now updates itself: from this version on, a newer release shows as a green **Update** entry at the foot of
the left rail, and one click downloads it, checks it and restarts into it.

**Windows:** this one last time, download `PokeUDS-windows.zip` and unzip it over your `PokeUDS` folder (0.1.3 has no
updater). Your `Bank`, `Data` and `received` folders are not in the zip and stay as they are.

## New

- **Updates:** the green Update entry appears when a newer release is out (Settings > Updates turns the check off,
  and Check now asks again). Updating replaces only PokeUDS's own program files (`PokeUDS.exe`, `_internal`,
  `LICENSE.txt`); the Bank and its GTS listings, your settings and received Pokemon are never touched, and a failed
  update puts the old version back. The download must match its checksum in `SHA256SUMS`.
- **The builder:** every pick box (Pokemon, moves, held item, ball, nature, gender, ability, language) can be typed
  in, and the list filters to what you type. The boxes share one size and line up; Move 3 and Move 4 share a row,
  Female OT sits beside the OT name and IDs. When PokeUDS stands in for a Virtual Console title, the builder starts
  in its language (German Crystal: German) without forcing it.
- **The GTS:** the want, level and Offering boxes take typing too; Browse starts on All formats (it started on
  `.pk7`, so `.pk6` listings did not show). When no relay answers, the page names each relay's error, and **Check
  the relays** lists every relay with its time or its error.
- **Mystery Gift:** the cards' sprites no longer make the page flicker and jump back to the top while they
  download; a bar shows their progress the first time.
- The Gen 7 key extractor's help and the README now say a decrypted CCI (`.cci`/`.3ds`) works as well as a CXI.

## Not tested yet

- The GTS over the public relays between two different computers.
- Japanese, Korean and non-English international Virtual Console titles on a console.
- X / Y with PokeUDS asking: did not trade on a console; a log from `Data\logs` of a try helps.
- An update from this version to the next (the first one this version can make).

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
- Only the ESP32-S3 is supported so far; firmware for the classic ESP32, C3 and C6 is on its way.
