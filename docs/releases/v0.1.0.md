The first alpha of PokeUDS: local wireless trades with retail 3DS Pokemon games, from a computer with an ESP32-S3
board (esp32-uds-bridge firmware 1.8) or a USB Wi-Fi adapter through ldnd.exe 0.0.5.

**Windows:** download `PokeUDS-windows.zip`, unzip it anywhere and run `PokeUDS/PokeUDS.exe`. You also need your
3DS's `aes_keys.txt` (the one Azahar uses), set once in Settings. Other systems: run it from source (README).

## What works

- **Virtual Console Red/Blue/Yellow and Gold/Silver/Crystal**: trades in the Trade Center, with the 3DS hosting.
- **X / Y**: PSS trades with the 3DS asking PokeUDS. Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire use X/Y's values.
- **Sun / Moon**: Festival Plaza Link Trades in both directions. Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon use Sun/Moon's values.
- A trade queue: several Pokemon in one session (up to six for the Game Boy games).
- The Bank: everything received, imported or built; PKHeX legality checks for one Pokemon or the whole bank; Time
  Capsule copies; migration of Gen 1/2 and Gen 6 Pokemon to Gen 7.
- Building legal Pokemon, Mystery Gift cards (Gen 6/7) and a card editor, through the PKHeX helper, which the app
  downloads from this release.
- Mystery Gift over local wireless, as an event distribution: Gen 6 cards to X/Y and ORAS, and Gen 7 cards to
  Sun/Moon and USUM, signed with the key Settings extracts from your own Gen 7 Local Distributor dump (verified on
  a retail Sun).
- esp32-uds-bridge 1.8 comes with the app: the Board page installs it on a board. Plugins and language packs.

## Not tested yet

- The Time Capsule from PokeUDS (a Gen 1 Pokemon from a `.pk2` to Red/Blue/Yellow, a `.pk1` to Gold/Silver/Crystal).
- Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon and Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire on a console.
- Several trades in one session on a console; a console declining or leaving the trade screen early.
- X / Y with PokeUDS asking; PokeUDS hosting the VC link.
- A Gen 2 Pokemon holding mail.
- A Gen 6 Mystery Gift distribution received by a console.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
