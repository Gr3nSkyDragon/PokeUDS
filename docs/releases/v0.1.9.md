The Gifts page now lists every Mystery Gift card PKHeX knows, items and key items included, and sorts them by the
language each event actually went out in. The GTS keeps finished trades out of the way, and the bank checks what you
receive by itself. Update from 0.1.8 with the green **Update** entry at the foot of the left rail; if it fails, download
`PokeUDS-windows.zip` and unzip it over your `PokeUDS` folder (your `Bank`, `Data` and `received` folders are not in
the zip and stay as they are).

## New

- **Every official card, not only Pokemon.** Items and key items are in the list: the Secret Key, Member Card and
  Oak's Letter, the Enigma Stone, the Pokéwalker courses (Yellow Forest, Winner's Path...), the Liberty Pass, the Eon
  Ticket, and the 3DS games' item and Battle Points cards, each in every language it went out in. (The Azure Flute is
  not in PKHeX's database; a card file of it opens with **Open a card**.)
- **Filters**, on one row with the search box: languages (tick several; **Universal** for cards meant for every
  language), **Pokemon/Eggs** or **Items/Key Items**, and for Pokemon, shiny and **Non-legendary** or
  **Legendary/Mythical**. The list follows each tick as you make it.
- **Languages from the event archive.** Each card's language comes from Project Pokemon's EventsGallery, which files
  every Wonder Card by the language it was distributed in; PokeUDS matched its files to PKHeX's cards and keeps only
  the languages (3,035 of 3,043 cards). Cards no longer show under English just because their title could not be read.
- **More card files:** `.wc4` (a Gen 4 card, its Pokemon encrypted or not) and `.wc5full` (a Gen 5 gift file) open in
  **Open a card** and in the card builder, alongside `.pcd`, `.myg`, `.pgf` and `.bin`.
- **The gift stays in view:** the card list (or the builder) scrolls on its own, and the gift panel beside it, with
  **Start sending**, stays where it is.
- **GTS:** Yours shows only what is still going; traded, taken-down and ended listings and offers wait under
  **History**, shown on request.
- **Bank:** a Pokemon from the received folder is checked with PKHeX on its own when it arrives. **Check** is for
  files you add by hand.
- `pokeuds gift list` takes the DS games too (`dp`, `pt`, `hgss`, `bw`, `b2w2`) and names what item and key-item cards
  give.

## Not tested yet

- Still from 0.1.8: firmware 2.1-uds on a board; PokeUDS on a Mac with a board on the ldn line; Gen 5 gifts and the
  builder's Gen 4 Pokemon and Gen 5 cards sent by PokeUDS itself; PokeUDS hosting the Time Capsule; Omega Ruby /
  Alpha Sapphire and Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon on a console; the classic ESP32; online trades and Touch Trade between two
  real 3DSs.
- Sending a Gen 4 key item (the Secret Key, Member Card, Oak's Letter) or a Pokéwalker course to a DS.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- After an X / Y trade, choosing to register PokeUDS's trainer as a friend fails on the 3DS: PokeUDS has no friend
  data to give it.
- Flashing any firmware erases the keys a board stores. PokeUDS doesn't use them, but Azahar and uds-esp32-setup users
  have to store theirs again.
- A custom card's Pokemon matches no real event, so PKHeX flags it ("Unable to match to a Mystery Gift").
- In a very narrow window the Gifts page's filter boxes narrow until their labels are cut short.
- The Windows app is unsigned: Windows may warn about an unknown publisher on first start (More info, Run anyway).
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
