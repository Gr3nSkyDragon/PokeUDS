A fixes release: the Gifts page's language filter takes every click again, the GTS stops saying no relay answered
when they did, and the Mac app no longer puts a second, stuck PokeUDS icon in the Dock. Update from 0.1.9 with the
green **Update** entry at the foot of the left rail; if it fails, download `PokeUDS-windows.zip` and unzip it over
your `PokeUDS` folder (your `Bank`, `Data` and `received` folders are not in the zip and stay as they are).

## Fixed

- **Gifts, languages:** the language menu ticks a language wherever its row is clicked and stays open, so several can
  be ticked in one go; each tick shows at once and the list follows when you pause. The box shows no sideways
  scrollbar when it is narrower than its text.
- **Gifts, Universal removed:** it did not sort cards the way it should. A card whose language cannot be told is
  listed when no language is ticked; one the archive files under every language is listed under each.
- **GTS:** with nothing waiting on the relays, a check says so instead of "No relay answered" (the relays had
  answered).
- **Mac:** the app's own process is marked a background app, so the Dock shows one PokeUDS icon, not a second one
  that bounced and then reported "Application Not Responding".
- The release builds use GitHub's Node 24 actions, and the Apple Silicon build runs on `macos-15`.

## Not tested yet

  Alpha Sapphire and Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon on a console; the classic ESP32; online trades and Touch Trade between two

- Sending a Gen 4 key item (the Secret Key, Member Card, Oak's Letter) or a Pokéwalker course to a DS.
- The Mac app's single Dock icon, on a Mac (the build checks the setting is there).

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- After an X / Y trade, choosing to register PokeUDS's trainer as a friend fails on the 3DS: PokeUDS has no friend
  data to give it.
- Flashing any firmware erases the keys a board stores. PokeUDS doesn't use them, but Azahar and uds-esp32-setup users
  have to store theirs again.
- A custom card's Pokemon matches no real event, so PKHeX flags it ("Unable to match to a Mystery Gift").
- In a very narrow window the Gifts page's filter boxes narrow until their labels are cut short.
- The packaged app keeps its bank (and GTS listings) in the `Bank` folder beside it; running from source keeps them
  in your user profile. Listings made in one do not show in the other.
- The Windows app is unsigned: Windows may warn about an unknown publisher on first start (More info, Run anyway).
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
