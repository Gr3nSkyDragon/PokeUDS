PokeUDS in eight more languages, and the bank can now send a Pokemon on to Sword/Shield. Update from 0.2.2 with the
green **Update** entry at the foot of the left rail; if it fails, download `PokeUDS-windows.zip` and unzip it over your
`PokeUDS` folder (your `Bank`, `Data` and `received` folders are not in the zip and stay as they are).

## New

- **Languages:** Settings, **App language** now offers Japanese, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Korean, and
  Simplified and Traditional Chinese, each shown in its own language with a "machine translation" tag in that language
  ("Français · Traduction automatique"). They are machine translations that use the games' own terms and official game
  titles; tell us where one reads wrong. Logs, file names and the command line stay English. A language pack for the
  same language (Plugins) lays over the built-in one: its strings win, the rest stays machine-translated.
- **Escape Rope (Bank):** the button at the end of the filter row lists everything Sword/Shield can take, and makes a
  `.pk8` the way Pokemon Bank and HOME would: a `.pk1`, `.pk2` or `.pk6` goes through its `.pk7` copy first, then your
  Builder trainer becomes its handler and it gets a HOME tracker. The original stays in the bank. Export the `.pk8` and
  take it into PKHeX or a Switch save; PokeUDS doesn't trade with a Switch.
- **Origin marks:** a `.pk7` or `.pk8` record shows its **Origin mark** (Game Boy, Kalos pentagon or clover), checked
  through every step by PKHeX.

## Changed

- **ESP32-S2:** no longer alpha, after a Gen 6 trade on a Lolin S2 Mini.
- **Board page:** shorter text, with the background moved to `docs/hardware.md`.

## Not tested yet

- A `.pk8` from Escape Rope in a real Switch save (PKHeX finds them legal).
- Still from 0.2.2: the rebuilt S3, C3, C6 and classic ESP32 images on a board; the S2 with Gen 7, ORAS, Virtual
  Console, DS gifts, and on a Mac; file dialogs and installing firmware in the Mac app, on a Mac; PokeUDS on a Mac with
  a board on the ldn line; Gen 5 gifts and the builder's Gen 4 Pokemon and Gen 5 cards sent by PokeUDS itself; PokeUDS
  hosting the Time Capsule; Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire and Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon on a console; the classic ESP32;
  online trades and Touch Trade between two real 3DSs; sending a Gen 4 key item or a Pokéwalker course to a DS; the Mac
  app's single Dock icon, on a Mac.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- The translations are machine-made: some wording may be off.
- An S2 cannot be reset into download mode by PokeUDS: use its BOOT and RESET buttons for every install.
- After an X / Y trade, choosing to register PokeUDS's trainer as a friend fails on the 3DS: PokeUDS has no friend
  data to give it.
- Flashing any firmware erases the keys a board stores. PokeUDS doesn't use them, but Azahar and uds-esp32-setup users
  have to store theirs again.
- A custom card's Pokemon matches no real event, so PKHeX flags it ("Unable to match to a Mystery Gift").
- In a very narrow window the Gifts page's filter boxes narrow until their labels are cut short.
- The packaged app keeps its bank (and GTS listings) in the `Bank` folder beside it; running from source keeps them
  in your user profile. Listings made in one do not show in the other.
- The Windows app is unsigned: Windows may warn about an unknown publisher on first start (More info, Run anyway).
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
