PokeUDS now sends Mystery Gifts to the DS games, Gen 4 and Gen 5, over the same board and firmware it trades with: the
Gifts page's new **Nintendo DS** game sends an official card, or one you build, as a store distribution did. Online
trades and Touch Trade arrive as experimental features. Update from 0.1.6 with the green **Update** entry at the foot
of the left rail; if it fails, download `PokeUDS-windows.zip` and unzip it over your `PokeUDS` folder (your `Bank`,
`Data` and `received` folders are not in the zip and stay as they are).

## New

- **DS Mystery Gifts over local wireless (Gen 4 / 5).** Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold, SoulSilver, Black,
  White, Black 2 and White 2 receive PKHeX's official cards in DS beacons, as a store distribution sent them: on the
  DS, Mystery Gift, Receive Gift, then the wireless option. The board needs no new firmware (2.0-uds sends the
  beacons as they are), and no console keys are involved. Ported from Shinygati's
  [PokeLDN-Bluetooth](https://github.com/Shinygati/PokeLDN-Bluetooth) fork of pokeldn.
  - Working: Gen 4 on a retail DS, official cards and a custom item card made with the builder below. Shinygati has
    confirmed Gen 5, and custom cards of both generations made with their own tools.
  - Choose the cartridge's language beside Start sending: the game ignores a gift for another.
- **A Gen 4 / 5 card builder.** Build your own now works for the DS games: a card giving a Pokemon (Gen 5 also an
  egg) or one item, exported as the `.pcd`/`.pgf` card or the `.myg`/`.bin` gift file, and sent from the page. Gen 4
  Pokemon can be never shiny (the game rolls them), always shiny (with the gender, nature and ability you choose) or
  a fixed PID; Gen 5 sets IVs, nature, gender and ability on the card. **Customize** opens an official DS card in it.
- **A board on another USB port is followed.** When no board is on the saved port and exactly one ESP32 is plugged
  in, every run uses that one and saves its port, and the run's log says so. With several boards PokeUDS does not
  guess, and a port given on the command line is used as given.
- **Trade page:** the role choice reads **Joins** or **Hosts** under a "PokeUDS" heading on every game, named for
  what the player sees (Joins: the 3DS starts the trade).
- **Demo consoles** for Sun / Moon and Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon (`--radio demo:sun`, and in the Debug page), so every
  generation can be tried with one 3DS.
- The two gift senders end with a one-line reason when the board or the card is missing, not a traceback.

## Experimental

- **Online trades:** X / Y, ORAS, Sun / Moon, USUM and the Virtual Console games trade live with a player far away,
  each 3DS beside its own PokeUDS and board, through public relays (the **Online** switch and a room code; each 3DS
  trades only once both have said yes). Tested in simulation; **not yet tried with two real 3DSs**
  (`docs/online.md`). An online trade keeps no copy of what your 3DS sent.
- **Touch Trade**, for trade evolutions: PokeUDS offers back whatever the 3DS offers, so the 3DS receives its own
  Pokemon in the same trade and evolves it (the **Touch Trade** switch, or `--touch-trade`).

## Removed

- **The DS Wi-Fi server** (`pokeuds wfc`, `docs/ds_wifi.md`), which stood in for Nintendo WFC and needed a Wii
  certificate dump and an open access point; it was never confirmed on a DS. DS gifts go over local wireless instead,
  and `requirements-wfc.txt` and the `tlslite-ng` extra go with it.

## Not tested yet

- Gen 5 gifts and this builder's Gen 4 Pokemon and Gen 5 cards sent by PokeUDS itself; the European Gen 4 game ids
  (French, German, Italian, Spanish).
- PokeUDS hosting the Time Capsule (a Gen 1 Pokemon offered to Gold / Silver / Crystal with PokeUDS hosting).
- Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire and Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon on a console (they use X / Y's and Sun / Moon's values).
- The classic ESP32.
- Japanese, Korean and non-English international Virtual Console titles on a console.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- A custom card's Pokemon matches no real event, so PKHeX flags it ("Unable to match to a Mystery Gift").
- The Windows app is unsigned: Windows may warn about an unknown publisher on first start (More info, Run anyway).
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
