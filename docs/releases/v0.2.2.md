ESP32-S2 boards join as an alpha, contributed by cerealcommas: a Lolin S2 Mini has traded Gen 6 with it. Also a Gen 4
goods card that names its goods, and the firmware PokeUDS installs rebuilt from VC-Adapter's merged source. Update from
0.2.1 with the green **Update** entry at the foot of the left rail; if it fails, download `PokeUDS-windows.zip` and
unzip it over your `PokeUDS` folder (your `Bank`, `Data` and `received` folders are not in the zip and stay as they
are).

## New

- **ESP32-S2 boards (alpha)**, such as the Lolin S2 Mini or a Flipper Wi-Fi Developer Board, by cerealcommas. PokeUDS
  includes `esp32-uds-bridge-2.1-uds-esp32s2.bin` and the Board page installs it. The S2 is installed from its download
  mode: hold **BOOT**, press **RESET**, release BOOT, then **Look again** and **Install 2.1-uds** on the download port.
  Press RESET when it finishes, then **Look again** and **Check** on the board's new port. Every install starts that
  way. A pokeldn S2 (which uses the same USB id) is left alone. Confirmed: a Gen 6 trade on an S2 Mini.

## Fixed

- **Gifts, Gen 4 goods:** card 0045 (ルカリオわくわくフェア プレゼント) said only "Goods"; it names what it gives, the
  Lucario Doll.

## Changed

- **Firmware:** the 2.1-uds images for the S3, C3, C6 and classic ESP32 are rebuilt from VC-Adapter's main with the
  S2 work merged. Still firmware 2.1-uds: a board already on it is told it is up to date and needs nothing. The one
  change for those chips: if Wi-Fi cannot start its event loop (out of memory), the board says so with its free memory
  instead of failing later.

## Not tested yet

- The S2 with Gen 7, X / Y beyond Gen 6, Virtual Console, DS gifts, and on a Mac.
- Still from 0.2.1: file dialogs and installing firmware in the Mac app, on a Mac; PokeUDS on a Mac with a board on the
  ldn line; Gen 5 gifts and the builder's Gen 4 Pokemon and Gen 5 cards sent by PokeUDS itself; PokeUDS hosting the

  Dock icon, on a Mac.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- An S2 cannot be reset into download mode by PokeUDS: use its BOOT and RESET buttons for every install.
- After an X / Y trade, choosing to register PokeUDS's trainer as a friend fails on the 3DS: PokeUDS has no friend
  data to give it.
- Flashing any firmware erases the keys a board stores. PokeUDS doesn't use them, but Azahar and uds-esp32-setup users
  have to store theirs again.
- A custom card's Pokemon matches no real event, so PKHeX flags it ("Unable to match to a Mystery Gift").
- In a very narrow window the Gifts page's filter boxes narrow until their labels are cut short.
- The packaged app keeps its bank (and GTS listings) in the `Bank` folder beside it; running from source keeps them
  in your user profile. Listings made in one do not show in the other.
- The Windows app is unsigned: Windows may warn about an unknown publisher on first start (More info, Run anyway).
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
