PokeUDS is now free software under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0** (`LICENSE`). This alpha also
brings the GTS, every language of the Virtual Console games, and a builder that repairs what PKHeX refuses.

**Windows:** unzip over your old `PokeUDS` folder, or anywhere new. Everything the app keeps now stays in its own
folder: your Pokemon in `Bank`, its settings, sprites and plugins in `Data` (hidden), and received files in
`received`. Nothing is written to your user profile; what an older version kept in `%APPDATA%\PokeUDS` is copied
over on the first start and left where it was. Run `PokeUDS.exe` from Explorer.

## New

- **Licence:** AGPL-3.0. pokeldn's code can now be used here with its notice; Lorenzooone's PokemonGB_Online_Trades
  (MIT) supplies the Japanese and Korean Game Boy text and layouts.
- **The GTS:** list a banked Pokemon against one to three wants, browse and offer on other players' listings, bank to
  bank over public Nostr relays. A trade settles when the lister's app next opens. Only Pokemon PKHeX finds legal go
  up, and a Game Boy Pokemon's language goes with it.
- **Virtual Console in every language:** all 39 titles, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese (Green
  too) and Korean Gold and Silver, each with its own trade layout. A Pokemon from one language is offered to a
  console of another with its names carried over; Japanese `.pk1`/`.pk2` files; the language kept in the bank and
  through migration to Gen 7. Fixes the old bug that joined Japanese Gold, Silver and Crystal as international games.
- **The builder:**
  - Import paste (Showdown and Smogon sets, whole teams) beside Build one; Build and save in Bank.
  - IVs and EVs, DVs and stat experience in Gen 1/2, and Hidden Power.
  - Language: the game a Pokemon comes from, names in that language, true Japanese and Korean Game Boy records.
  - Repairs: a trade evolution is traded once and back (Aromatisse, Gengar, Machamp), an evolution gets the level it
    needs, egg and event moves their relearn moves, gender-locked evolutions (Froslass, Salazzle, Shedinja) their
    right pre-evolution.
  - When nothing legal has everything asked for, the closest legal Pokemon, with each change listed: Keep this one,
    or Back to editing.
- **PKHeX is part of the app:** nothing to download or build.
- **Logs:** each run's log is saved in `Data\logs`, the newest 30 kept.

## Not tested yet

- The GTS over the public relays.
- Japanese, Korean and non-English international Virtual Console titles on a console (only English Red, Gold and
  Silver have been).
- X / Y with PokeUDS asking: tried once on a console, and it did not trade. A log from `Data\logs` of a try helps.
- PokeUDS hosting the VC link; Ultra Sun / Ultra Moon and Omega Ruby / Alpha Sapphire on a console.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
- Online trades (live, between two players) are not here yet.
