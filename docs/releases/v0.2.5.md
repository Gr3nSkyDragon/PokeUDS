The builder no longer lags. Update from 0.2.4 with the green **Update** entry at the foot of the left rail; if it
fails, download `PokeUDS-windows.zip` and unzip it over your `PokeUDS` folder (your `Bank`, `Data` and `received`
folders are not in the zip and stay as they are).

## Fixed

- **The builder (Trade page, Build one) is quick again.** It lagged in recent releases: a pick could take a second
  to show, and a quick second click sometimes put Pikachu back. Its window held
  about 480 MB for the builder's long lists (the species, the held items and the four move boxes, about 7,000 entries
  between them); it now holds about 225 MB. What changed:
  - Picking a Pokemon changes its sprite and empties the move boxes, and nothing else: the builder is no longer
    redrawn after every pick.
  - A long list reaches the window when your pointer first comes over its box, not with the builder, so opening the
    builder is quick and only the boxes you use carry their whole list.
  - Each pick, key or click in a box no longer makes PokeUDS look over the whole page.
- The same boxes on the GTS page get the same speed-up.

## Changed

- **Bank:** a **Gen 8** filter, for the Sword/Shield `.pk8` copies Escape Rope makes. The bank folder keeps each
  Pokemon in a folder for its generation (Gen 1, Gen 2, Gen 6, Gen 7, Gen 8); a bank from an older version is sorted
  into them once, the first time it opens, and trade queues follow the files.
- PokeUDS stays on Flet 1.1.2: the builder's lag was its own, and it measured the same on Flet 1.0.5.

## Tested

- The builder on Windows 10 with the built app: picks from the list and by typing, the arrow and the box opening the
  lists, typing right after a click, and the window's memory with the builder open on Flet 1.1.2 and 1.0.5.

## Known issues

- An alpha: expect rough edges, and keep backups of anything you trade away.
- The translations are machine-made: some wording may be off.
- In the builder, typing part of a name and leaving the box with Tab picks the first match, but its short filtered
  list stays open until your next click.
- A copy of PokeUDS's folder is a second player with its own bank; a moved `PokeUDS.exe` opens the one that started
  last.
- An S2 cannot be reset into download mode by PokeUDS: use its BOOT and RESET buttons for every install.
- After an X / Y trade, choosing to register PokeUDS's trainer as a friend fails on the 3DS: PokeUDS has no friend
  data to give it.
- Flashing any firmware erases the keys a board stores. PokeUDS doesn't use them, but Azahar and uds-esp32-setup users
  have to store theirs again.
- A custom card's Pokemon matches no real event, so PKHeX flags it ("Unable to match to a Mystery Gift").
- In a very narrow window the Gifts page's filter boxes narrow until their labels are cut short.
- The packaged app keeps its bank (and GTS listings) in the `Bank` folder beside it; running from source keeps them
  in your user profile. Listings made in one do not show in the other.
- The Windows app is unsigned: Windows may warn about an unknown publisher on first start (More info, Run anyway).
- Started from a sandboxed shell (a terminal inside some tools), the app runs with restricted rights and cannot write
  its files: start it from Explorer.
